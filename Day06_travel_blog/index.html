Travel Tips for Japan

  • Get a transportation IC card for convenient travel.
  • Carry some cash for places that do not accept cards.
  • Respect local customs and keep public places clean.
  • Check the weather before planning outdoor activities.
  • Learn a few basic Japanese phrases.

Travel Checklist

  1. Choose your destination.
  2. Plan your travel route.
  3. Check transportation options.
  4. Prepare your camera and essentials.
  5. Enjoy your journey!
